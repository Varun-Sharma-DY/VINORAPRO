## Stack

## Users

Established businesses with real operational complexity (manufacturing, construction, roofing and building materials, distribution, multi-department or multi-location companies). Two readers share the decision: an operations head who feels the daily pain, researches and shortlists; and the owner or MD, who decides and signs off. The site must work for both. The job: judge whether Vinora can build software around how their business actually runs, then book a discovery call.

## Product Purpose

The website exists to generate qualified discovery-call bookings for Vinora LLP, not to showcase a catalogue or serve as a generic agency site. Success is a qualified enquiry from a business that justifies a custom software engagement.

## Positioning

Vinora builds ERP and automation around the business's actual working methods, down to individual functions, permissions, notifications and operational details, instead of making the business adapt to predefined modules. A packaged-ERP vendor cannot truthfully claim this. "Anything and everything" describes the approach to customisation, not an unlimited guarantee.

## Operating Context

The buyer typically runs on Excel sheets, manual registers and disconnected tools. The conversion path is website, then discovery call, then requirement understanding, proposed solution, demo, commercial discussion. A demo is never the first step. Primary CTA: Book a Discovery Call. Secondary CTA: Discuss Your Requirements.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Custom ERP, business automation, custom software and integrations (see sections 3 and 6 below). No fixed delivery timelines or pricing without approval. No certifications or partnerships claimed without verification. Pages proposed in ARCHITECTURE.md: Home, Services, About, Contact; Portfolio deferred. Open: booking integration, hosting and deployment, final page structure.

## Brand Commitments

Existing blue-and-white identity. Voice: capable and confident without arrogance; practical business language; no hype, aggressive sales language or empty corporate jargon. Never positioned as merely a website agency.

## Evidence on Hand

The owner confirms a logo file, exact brand HEX colours and contact details (business email, phone, booking details) exist, but none are in the repository yet; they must be supplied before use and never guessed. Absent and must not be fabricated: client testimonials, customer statistics, project results, case studies, portfolio work, pricing, certifications, partnerships, company history and headcount.

## Product Principles

1. Customisation is the claim; every section proves it with concrete operational detail, not adjectives.
2. Speak in the buyer's practical language (registers, stock, orders, approvals), not digital-transformation jargon.
3. Credibility comes from precision and process clarity, not invented proof.
4. Discovery call first; the path to it is always one clear step away.
5. Every section earns its place by answering one of the content-principle questions.

## 1. Company Overview

**Company:** Vinora LLP

**Primary Specialisation:** Custom ERP Development and Business Automation.

Vinora builds customised software solutions that simplify business operations, automate repetitive processes, and give businesses better control over their operations.

The company specialises in developing ERP systems around the actual working methods of individual businesses rather than forcing businesses to adapt to predefined software structures.

## 2. Core Value Proposition

**Absolute Customisation. Simplified Operations.**

Every business operates differently. Its software should reflect that.

Unlike conventional ERP platforms that provide predefined modules and workflows, Vinora develops systems that can be customised down to individual functions, permissions, notifications, operational details and business requirements.

The objective is not simply to digitise existing processes. It is to simplify and improve how the business operates.

## 3. What We Offer

### Custom ERP Development
- Complete business management systems.
- Inventory and stock management.
- Order and sales management.
- Finance and billing.
- Workforce and attendance management.
- Project and site management.
- Role-based access control.
- Custom dashboards and reporting.
- Operational tracking and monitoring.
- Custom notifications and approval workflows.

These are examples of capabilities, not a fixed list of mandatory modules.

### Business Automation
- Repetitive task automation.
- Workflow automation.
- Notification automation.
- Payment integrations.
- Third-party API integrations.
- Data synchronisation.
- Custom internal tools.
- Process-specific automation.

### Custom Software Solutions

Software developed to solve specific operational problems that existing platforms cannot adequately address.

## 4. Our Differentiator

Our primary competitive advantage is customisation combined with simplification.

Traditional ERP platforms often require businesses to adapt their operations to the software.

Vinora takes the opposite approach.

We understand how the business operates and build software around those requirements.

Customisation can extend to extremely specific operational details.

For example:
- Different notifications for different business functions.
- Payment portal integration.
- Tracking which pickup vehicle carries a particular order.
- Recording departure times.
- Optional operational notes.
- Custom permissions and workflows.

These examples demonstrate the level of operational detail possible within our systems.

**Our philosophy: If a business has a genuine operational requirement, we should explore how software and automation can solve it.**

"Anything and everything" describes our approach to customisation, not an unlimited or unrealistic guarantee.

## 5. Target Customers

Vinora primarily targets established businesses with meaningful operational complexity and sufficient purchasing capacity.

Potential customer segments include:
- Manufacturing and industrial businesses.
- Construction and infrastructure companies.
- Roofing and building-material businesses.
- Distribution and supply-chain businesses.
- Established companies with multiple departments or locations.
- Businesses experiencing operational inefficiencies due to disconnected systems.

We are not primarily targeting:
- Individuals looking for inexpensive websites.
- Very small businesses with limited software budgets.
- Customers looking for generic, off-the-shelf solutions.
- Businesses whose requirements do not justify a custom software engagement.

## 6. Problems We Solve

Our ideal customers may experience:
- Excessive dependence on Excel sheets and manual registers.
- Disconnected departments and information.
- Poor visibility into business operations.
- Repetitive administrative work.
- Delayed reporting and decision-making.
- Difficulty tracking orders, inventory, employees or projects.
- Lack of accountability.
- Multiple software tools that do not communicate with each other.
- Existing ERP platforms that cannot accommodate their actual workflows.

The website must communicate these problems in practical business language.

Avoid exaggerated claims and generic digital-transformation terminology.

## 7. Our Approach

The customer journey should communicate the following process:

1. **Discovery:** Understand the business, its operations and its challenges.
2. **Analysis:** Identify inefficiencies and determine what should be automated or digitised.
3. **Custom Solution Design:** Define the ERP structure, modules, workflows and integrations.
4. **Development:** Build the system around the approved requirements.
5. **Testing and Deployment:** Validate functionality and introduce the system into the business.
6. **Ongoing Support:** Maintain, improve and extend the software as business requirements evolve.

Do not promise fixed delivery timelines without an actual project assessment.

## 12. Content Principle

Every section of the website should answer at least one question:

- What problem does Vinora solve?
- Why is custom software better for this particular requirement?
- What makes Vinora different?
- How does the engagement work?
- Why should a business trust the process?
- What should the visitor do next?

If a section does not serve a meaningful purpose, reconsider whether it belongs on the website.
